# ⟁ ABOUT SLURP

> **The anti-bloat manifesto for media extraction.**

---

## 🎯 The Philosophy

Most online video downloaders are broken:
- 50 flashing scam banner ads.
- Fake "Download" buttons tricking you into installing malware.
- 15-second artificial countdown timers.
- Clunky interfaces that break on mobile.

**SLURP was built as the exact opposite.**

It follows a single, unbreakable rule:
> **Do 1 thing flawlessly: paste a link $\rightarrow$ download the media. Nothing more, nothing less.**

---

## ⚡ Core Pillars

### 1. Zero Fluff, Pure Utility
- **Videos**: Direct download of original high-definition `.mp4` files with watermarks removed.
- **Slideshows**: Instant in-memory bundling of all full-resolution photos and original audio into a single `.zip` archive (compiled in ~10ms with zero CPU overhead).

### 2. Weird-Minimalist Brutalist Aesthetic
- High-contrast obsidian void backdrop (`#070708`) with electric acid-lime accents (`#d4ff28`).
- Monolithic typography, tactile mechanical micro-interactions, and responsive layout for every screen size.

### 3. Ludicrous Speed & Zero-Buffer Streaming
- Multi-endpoint parallel racing (`Promise.any`) across global CDNs.
- Direct socket piping: bytes stream straight to your disk without being buffered in server RAM or browser memory.

### 4. Built for Humans and Autonomous AI Agents
- Clean REST API endpoints with predictable JSON schemas and direct binary stream attachments.
- Ready for integration with LLMs, scrapers, and automation bots.
