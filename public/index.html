Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about downloading social media videos and slideshows.

How do I download TikTok photo slideshows as a ZIP file? +

Simply copy the URL of the TikTok photo slideshow and paste it into SLURP. The engine immediately resolves all high-definition slide images and bundles them with the original soundtrack into a single .ZIP file. Clicking download immediately saves the complete ZIP to your device.

Does SLURP remove watermarks from TikTok videos? +

Yes. SLURP bypasses the post-processed player and fetches the source media stream directly from the content delivery network (CDN), producing clean, original-bitrate MP4 files with zero TikTok logos or username watermarks.

Is SLURP free to use, and do I need an account? +

SLURP is 100% free with unlimited downloads. You do not need to register, install browser extensions, or provide any personal information. There are zero ads, popups, or subscriptions.

How do I save downloaded videos on iPhone (iOS)? +

On iOS (Safari), paste your link into SLURP and tap Download. Safari will prompt you to download the .MP4 file or .ZIP archive. Tap the download icon in Safari’s address bar, select the downloaded file, tap the Share icon, and choose "Save Video" to add it directly to your Photos Camera Roll.

Can I extract just the soundtrack or audio? +

When downloading a TikTok slideshow, SLURP automatically includes the full isolated soundtrack.mp3 inside the ZIP archive. For videos, the original uncompressed audio track is preserved within the MP4 file.

Why does SLURP say my link could not be resolved? +

Make sure the media is on a public account. Private videos, geo-restricted content, or deleted posts cannot be resolved. Double-check that your URL starts with http:// or https://.
